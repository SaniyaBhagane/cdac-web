Not bragging, but ...: Trump's fresh bid for Nobel Peace Prize

Donald Trump has once again thrown his hat into the ring for the Nobel Peace Prize, citing his efforts in brokering peace deals in the Middle East. The former president's latest bid comes amid a backdrop of political controversy and ongoing debates about his legacy.

Trump's nomination has sparked a wave of reactions from both supporters and critics. Advocates argue that his administration's role in facilitating agreements between Israel and several Arab nations deserves recognition, while detractors question the long-term impact of these deals and the overall approach to foreign policy.
